The challenge

As an early-stage AI startup, MarketCrunch had no content infrastructure: no defined content types, no research process for understanding what the audience responded to, and no consistent distribution logic across platforms. Content was being produced ad hoc — and performing that way.

What I did

1. Built a structured research pipeline. I systematized audience and creator insight generation through social listening, competitor mapping, and A/B testing — so every content decision started from evidence, not instinct.

2. Designed a scalable content workflow. I defined a content-type taxonomy, creative format logic, and a distribution strategy tailored to Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, plus the asset coordination system to keep production consistent across all three.

3. Found and fixed the real growth blocker. Data analysis pointed to watch-through rate as the bottleneck — viewers were dropping off before the value landed. I redesigned video hooks and creative pacing across 40+ short-form videos.

4. Worked cross-functionally. I partnered with engineering to translate audience insights into creative strategy, and led a brand identity refresh that was adopted across web, social, and product channels.
