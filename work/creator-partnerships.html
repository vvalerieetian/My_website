Lancôme x Xiaoyuzhou: creator strategy that got adopted, fully

For Lancôme's collaboration with Xiaoyuzhou (小宇宙, China's leading podcast platform), I owned the creator-advertiser collaboration strategy end to end:

  • Defined creator selection criteria and mapped each criterion to a specific campaign objective — so every recommendation could be defended with logic, not taste
  • Optimized the creative briefing journey between brand and creators, reducing back-and-forth and misalignment
  • Result: 100% proposal adoption — every recommendation was accepted by the client

L'Oréal Paris Fashion Week: creator monetization by design

For L'Oréal's Paris Fashion Week campaign, I designed a KOL-focused creative strategy covering creator segmentation, content formats, and channel strategy. I delivered 3 tailored proposals built to enable creator monetization through branded content partnerships — aligning what creators wanted to earn with what the brand wanted to say.

Fixing the machine behind the campaigns

Great creator strategy dies in a slow approval cycle. I identified bottlenecks across advertiser-creator-agency touchpoints and designed SOP-based workflow solutions that reduced turnaround by 10+ hours per month.

I also localized 20+ bilingual proposals for international luxury brands, achieving 95% client satisfaction — localization here meaning cultural translation of strategy, not just language.
