iPhone 17 post-launch perception → AI-powered campaign

A USC Annenberg project (PR523) that runs the full loop I use in industry: mine social data to find the narrative problem, then design the campaign that fixes it — with AI as a strategy partner, not a shortcut.

Role
Lead — Data & Campaign Strategy
Context
USC Annenberg · PR523
Tools
Mention · ChatGPT · Gemini
Subject
Apple iPhone 17 launch

Part 1: What was the internet actually saying?

I led data collection via Mention and built a sentiment analysis pipeline to surface audience pain points around Apple's post-launch narrative — separating what Apple thought the story was from what audiences were actually discussing.

The findings were translated into localized go-to-market recommendations: where the narrative was leaking, which audience segments were driving negative sentiment, and what messaging would close the gap.

Part 2: The AI-prompted campaign

I independently devised an AI-prompted campaign using ChatGPT and Gemini, designing a product-first storytelling framework built on:

  • Community-powered UGC — turning real users into the narrative engine
  • Creator collaboration profiles — defining which creator archetypes could authentically carry each message
  • Product-first storytelling — leading with what the product does for people, not spec sheets
